Ganito siya: hindi si Render ang mag-o-open ng local Chromium mo. Hindi niya kaya kontrolin yung browser sa Windows PC mo remotely by itself.

Ang tamang architecture ay:

Discord / Render
      ↓
v!jobstreet
      ↓
Render generates ONE-TIME signed link
      ↓
ikaw mag-open nung link sa Windows PC mo
      ↓
download/run maliit na local connector
      ↓
LOCAL connector launches Chromium sa PC mo
      ↓
JobStreet opens automatically
      ↓
ikaw manual Google login / 2FA / CAPTCHA
      ↓
local connector gets Playwright storage_state
      ↓
HTTPS upload pabalik sa Render
      ↓
Render encrypts + stores sa Supabase
      ↓
JobStreet = READY

So Render = server/coordinator, while your PC = temporary authentication device.

After successful login, hindi na kailangan naka-open PC mo. Yung authenticated session nasa encrypted database na, then si Render na gagamit nun sa scheduled JobStreet scanning.

Yung connector puwedeng .ps1 / .cmd na automatically runs something like:

python -m src.jobstreet_auth --token <one-time-token>

Then Chromium opens locally, hindi sa Render.

Bakit hindi puwedeng normal Chrome tab lang?

Kung ordinary jobstreet.com tab lang sa Chrome mo, si Render hindi makakakuha ng authenticated cookies/session dahil nasa browser mo yun at protected by browser security.

Kailangan yung login mangyari inside a browser context na controlled by the local Playwright helper. Doon niya makukuha safely yung resulting storage_state, then upload to Render.

So ang magandang final UX:

Render gives you link → one click/run locally → Chromium opens → login → READY → PC no longer needed.

Hindi Browserless, walang mabilis na remote session timeout, at one-time local authentication lang until mag-expire yung JobStreet session.
